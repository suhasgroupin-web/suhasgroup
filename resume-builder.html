Professional CV writing guide
  • Use a clear job title that matches the position you want.
  • Write a short summary focused on your experience and strengths.
  • Describe work achievements with action words and measurable results where possible.
  • List education, skills, training and languages accurately.
  • Use a clear, professional photo and check spelling before downloading.
  • Keep personal identification numbers private unless an employer genuinely requires them.
