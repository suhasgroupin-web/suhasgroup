RELIGION & FAITH

Learn About World Religions

Respectful, educational introductions. Beliefs and practices vary within each tradition.

☪️ Islam

Islam teaches belief in one God, Allah, and follows the Qur’an and the teachings of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).

Core learning

  • Six articles of faith and the Five Pillars: Shahada, Salah, Zakat, Sawm and Hajj.
  • Five daily prayers: Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha.
  • Prayer learning: Wudu, prayer times, Qibla, intention, recitation, standing, bowing and prostration.
  • Common Hanafi Fard rak‘ah counts: Fajr 2, Dhuhr 4, Asr 4, Maghrib 3, Isha 4. Sunnah and Nafl rak‘ahs vary by school and practice; consult a trusted scholar for a complete guide.
  • Ethics: honesty, compassion, justice, charity and respect for others.

Learning sections

Islamic Beliefs & Five Pillars · How to Perform Salah · Five Daily Prayers & Rak‘ah Guide · Wudu Guide · Qur’an & Learning · Islamic Ethics.

🕉️ Hinduism

A diverse family of traditions with varied philosophies and devotional practices.

  • Key concepts: Dharma, Karma, Samsara and Moksha.
  • Practices: Puja, prayer, meditation, festivals, pilgrimage and service.
  • Texts include the Vedas, Upanishads and Bhagavad Gita.

Sections: Beliefs & Philosophy · Worship & Puja · Sacred Texts · Festivals · Ethics.

✝️ Christianity

Centred on the life and teachings of Jesus Christ and the Bible.

  • Core teachings include love of God and neighbour, faith, forgiveness and service.
  • Worship includes prayer, scripture reading, church services and sacraments or ordinances.
  • Major traditions include Catholic, Orthodox and Protestant communities.

Sections: Core Beliefs · Prayer & Worship · Bible Study · Church & Sacraments · Festivals.

☸️ Buddhism

Follows the Buddha’s teachings on understanding suffering and cultivating liberation.

  • Foundations: Four Noble Truths and the Noble Eightfold Path.
  • Practices include meditation, mindfulness, ethical conduct and wisdom.
  • Traditions include Theravada, Mahayana and Vajrayana.

Sections: Four Noble Truths · Eightfold Path · Meditation · Texts · Ethics.

☬ Sikhism

Teaches devotion to one God, equality, honest living and service to humanity.

  • Principles: Naam Japna, Kirat Karni and Vand Chakna.
  • The Guru Granth Sahib is the central scripture.
  • Practices include prayer, seva and langar.

Sections: Core Beliefs · Daily Prayers · Guru Granth Sahib · Seva & Langar · The Five Ks.

✡️ Judaism

An ancient monotheistic tradition rooted in Torah, covenant, community and ethical living.

  • Practices include prayer, Shabbat, festivals, study and acts of kindness.
  • Jewish communities include diverse traditions and interpretations.

Sections: Beliefs & Torah · Prayer & Synagogue · Shabbat & Festivals · Jewish Life · Ethics.

🪷 Jainism

Emphasises non-violence, truth, non-attachment and spiritual liberation.

  • Ahimsa (non-violence) is central.
  • Other principles include truthfulness, non-stealing, restraint and non-possession.
  • Practices may include meditation, fasting and study.

Sections: Core Principles · Five Vows · Ahimsa · Meditation · Festivals.
