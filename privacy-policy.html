Privacy Policy

SUHAS GROUP · Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SUHAS GROUP ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our website and any feature that connects to LinkedIn.

1. Information we may receive

If you choose to connect your LinkedIn account and authorize the permissions shown on LinkedIn's consent screen, we may receive the information and permissions needed for that feature. Depending on the permissions approved, this may include your LinkedIn member identifier, name, profile photo, email address, and permission to perform actions such as creating or managing content on your behalf or for an associated organization.

We only request and use information made available through the permissions you approve. We do not ask you to provide your LinkedIn password to us.

2. How we use information

We use authorized information only to provide, operate, secure, and improve the SUHAS GROUP features you choose to use; to authenticate your connection; and to carry out actions you explicitly initiate. We do not sell personal information.

3. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or rent LinkedIn member data. Information may be processed by service providers that support our website, hosting, database, and application operations, or disclosed where required by law. We do not use LinkedIn data for purposes unrelated to the feature you authorized.

4. Storage and security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Access tokens and other credentials, where used, should be stored securely and never shared publicly.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the feature, meet legitimate operational requirements, or comply with applicable law. You may ask us to disconnect your LinkedIn integration and request deletion of information associated with it by contacting us at the email below. You can also revoke the app's access from your LinkedIn account settings.

6. Third-party services

LinkedIn and other third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their independent privacy practices.

7. Children's privacy

Our LinkedIn integration is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact: suhasgroup.in@gmail.com.
